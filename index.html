My Approach

Let's start to explore my world

This is the beginning of my journey.

01 · Discover a problem

First, look for problems around you. Research them using online platforms and available resources. Analyse what the problem is and why it happens.

02 · Think and create

After understanding the problem, think of possible ideas. Try making a prototype and communicate with people around you. Ask friends, family and neighbours for help.

03 · Test and improve

A prototype helps you understand the problem and identify its merits and demerits. Use the available tools to find a practical solution and improve your design.

04 · Build and share

When you have developed a suitable solution, implement it as a real-life project. Present it to others, invite questions about its uses, identify problems and debug them. Communicating with society helps you learn and improve.

Thank you for exploring my journey!
